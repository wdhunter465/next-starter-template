## Commitments made in the sent emails

- To CMG: the club will refrain from publishing material where CMG approval may be required until CMG replies; it will not imply CMG or the Gehrig estate endorses it; it will remove content on a rights holder's request.
- To Live Like Lou: campaign details go to the foundation for review before the February 2027 announcement; the Givebutter campaign is to be revised under the foundation's account so donations go directly to it; Product Authority will donate memorabilia from the collection as prizes.

- To the Yankees and to MLB: the club will refrain from publishing material where the Yankees' permission may be required until it receives guidance; it will not imply the Yankees or MLB endorse or sponsor it; it will review and act on a rights holder's concern about published material. The 2027 fundraiser with the Live Like Lou Foundation was mentioned to both.

The first commitment is in force now. Until CMG answers, content that uses Lou Gehrig's name or likeness where CMG approval may be required stays unpublished. The archive's "not permitted" default already behaves this way; the commitment makes it a promise to a third party.

## CMG email as sent (licensing guidance request)

Subject: Licensing Guidance Request — Lou Gehrig Fan Club Website

> Hello Mark,
>
> I am writing on behalf of the Lou Gehrig Fan Club (LGFC), a fan-run social media community dedicated to honoring Lou Gehrig's legacy and supporting ALS awareness. Our new website is https://www.lougehrigfanclub.com.
>
> We understand that CMG Worldwide is the exclusive licensing agent for Lou Gehrig. We would appreciate written guidance on whether CMG approval is required for our use of Lou Gehrig's name and likeness on our non-commercial fan website and, if so, the scope and conditions of that approval.
>
> **Our website content**
>
> We collect and display photographs, historical stories, and memorabilia related to Lou Gehrig. We use material that is in the public domain, openly licensed, or used with permission from the relevant rights holder. We credit our sources and document permissions and their applicable terms before publication.
>
> Could you please clarify the following?
>
> 1. **Approval requirements:** Does CMG require approval for Lou Gehrig-related content on a non-commercial fan website such as ours? If requirements vary by type of content or use, please explain the distinctions.
> 2. **Scope and conditions:** If approval is required, which uses does it cover—for example, Lou Gehrig's name, likeness, biographical stories, photographs, or memorabilia images? Are there applicable fees, restrictions, or other conditions?
> 3. **Attribution:** Is there a preferred credit line or other acknowledgment CMG would like us to use?
> 4. **Appropriate contact:** If another person or department handles these questions, could you please direct us to the appropriate contact?
>
> **About the Lou Gehrig Fan Club**
>
> LGFC operates on a zero-budget, zero-profit basis. Membership is always free. We do not sell content or products, and we do not use advertising or affiliate links.
>
> When we host events, funds raised are used to offset event expenses, with any remaining funds donated to the ALS charity supported by the event. For example, LGFC hosted an event at the National Baseball Hall of Fame in Cooperstown in June 2023. Funds raised covered event expenses, and a donation was made to the ALS Cure Project. Mike Piscotty, a founder of the charity, attended as a guest of honor.
>
> In 2027, LGFC plans to host its first fundraising campaign in partnership with the Live Like Lou Foundation.
>
> We credit our sources, document permissions, and will promptly remove content if requested by the relevant rights holder. Our administrative contact is admin@lougehrigfanclub.com.
>
> We do not represent or imply that CMG Worldwide or the Gehrig estate endorses LGFC. We would, of course, be honored to explore the possibility of an endorsement or other relationship in the future, should that be of interest to CMG.
>
> Until we receive your guidance, we will refrain from publishing material where we believe CMG approval may be required.
>
> A brief reply—even confirmation that no approval is required for certain uses—would help us ensure that we proceed appropriately. We appreciate your time and assistance in helping us preserve and share Lou Gehrig's legacy responsibly.
>
> Sincerely,
> Bill Hunter
> Founder and Administrator, Lou Gehrig Fan Club

## Shared core wording for the Yankees, MLB and LouGehrig.com

These blocks repeat in every request. Use them as written so the answers are comparable, and change only the per-holder ask.

**Who we are.** "The Lou Gehrig Fan Club (LGFC) is a fan-run community dedicated to honoring Lou Gehrig's legacy and supporting ALS awareness. Our website is https://www.lougehrigfanclub.com."

**What the site does.** "We collect and display photographs, historical stories, and memorabilia related to Lou Gehrig. We use material that is in the public domain, openly licensed, or used with permission from the relevant rights holder, and we credit our sources and record permissions and their terms before publication."

**Zero-profit statement.** "LGFC operates on a zero-budget, zero-profit basis. Membership is free. We do not sell content or products, and we do not use advertising or affiliate links."

**Event and fundraiser facts.** The June 2023 Cooperstown event and the donation to the ALS Cure Project, as in the CMG email. The 2027 fundraiser sentence ("In 2027, LGFC plans to host its first fundraising campaign in partnership with the Live Like Lou Foundation") may be sent only after Product Authority confirms the fundraiser may be mentioned to that recipient; the public announcement is planned for 2027-02-01.

**Removal and contact.** "We will promptly remove content if requested by the relevant rights holder. Our administrative contact is admin@lougehrigfanclub.com."

**No endorsement.** "We do not represent or imply that [holder] endorses LGFC."

**Holding statement.** "Until we receive your guidance, we will refrain from publishing material where we believe your approval may be required." Send this to a holder only if the club is willing to keep the promise for that holder's material.

## Sent Yankees and MLB emails

Both emails use the shared core wording and four questions: copyright and permission requirements, team intellectual property, scope and conditions, and rights ownership and referrals. The Yankees email is addressed to the Yankees legal or licensing team through the customer service inbox. The MLB email went to three MLB departments.

**Wording error in the MLB email.** The MLB email was built from the Yankees email and still names the Yankees in its body: it asks for guidance "from the New York Yankees", asks whether "the Yankees organization" controls or licenses content, and promises to refrain from publishing where "the Yankees' permission may be required". Only the first line ("Hello MLB Photos, Legal or Licensing Team") and the subject refer to MLB. The effect: MLB staff may take the questions as meant for the team, and the holding promise, as worded, is made to the Yankees and not to MLB. Correction status: the email went out uncorrected (Product Authority, 2026-10-22). A follow-up in the same thread is still possible and is Product Authority's to send; until one is sent, read any MLB reply with this wording in mind and do not treat the holding promise as made to MLB.

Open points 1 and 2 below were settled in effect by the sends: the holding statement went to CMG, the Yankees and MLB, and the fundraiser sentence went to CMG, the Yankees and MLB. Treat them as the working practice unless Product Authority changes it.

## Informal contact with John Howell

Product Authority wrote to John Howell at a personal address on 2026-10-22, as an existing partner, with an update on the club and the planned archive (Library, Gallery, Memorabilia, newspaper-style site with automated rotation) and asked about copyright or licensing concerns and about material he, the Rip Van Winkle Foundation or the Lou Gehrig Society could share or authorize. The address is not recorded here.

What this exchange is and is not:

- It is a relationship contact. It states no scope of use, credit wording, removal promise or holding statement, and it does not mention the fundraiser.
- A friendly reply is not permission. If he offers material or says there is no concern, ask in writing for the scope in his words (which items, which uses, credit line, who has authority), and treat it as a normal request under the form-letter how-to.
- Three bodies are named. Confirm in writing which of them owns the material in question, and whether LouGehrig.com is run by one of them. Rights are recorded per source, so each body that grants anything gets its own record.
- Product Authority's mailbox holds the original. Forward any reply to the permission inbox or paste it onto #4368 so it can be archived.
